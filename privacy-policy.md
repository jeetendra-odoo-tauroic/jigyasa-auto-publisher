Privacy Policy — Jigyasa Auto Publisher

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Jigyasa Auto Publisher is a tool used to publish educational content to the Jigyasa Facebook Page.

This tool is designed to publish educational posts, captions, and images. It does not intentionally collect personal information from Facebook users.
